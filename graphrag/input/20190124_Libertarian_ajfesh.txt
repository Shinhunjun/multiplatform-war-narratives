Title: I am appalled at the eagerness to invade Venezuela and institute regime change

I am appalled at the eagerness to invade Venezuela and institute regime change For the record I am no supporter of Nicolas Maduro and think it would be best for the country for him to step down. But I am absolutely appalled at the willingness on reddit to take military action if the internal coup does not work. Isn't it obvious why the United States and other world powers are so interested in the politics of a country most people cant even find on a map? OIL. That's right. If you read up on geopolitical history you'll know that the U.S. has had its eye on Venezuela for a long time, ever since the government nationalized their oil in the late 1990s. Sort of like how we wanted and actually succeeded in regime change in Iran for the same reasons. 

I dont know much about the opposition leader only that he is friendly to the west while Maduro clearly isn't. But we have seen this story before where a violent dictator is ousted by revolutionaries (often backed by the West) only to form a government just as corrupt and oppressive as the last. Meet the new boss same as the old boss. Like in Vietnam where no matter how ruthless Ho Chi Minh was the western backed South Vietnamese government wasn't any better. 

It absolutely disturbs me to see such support for potential military action in Venezuela. And as we all know about the mainstream media, no matter what station, they're always on board for a good ole fashioned regime change. I hope that we won't get fooled again but I'm absolutely terrified we will.
