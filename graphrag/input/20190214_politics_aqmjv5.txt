Title: Senate Votes to Confirm William Barr as Next Attorney General

Senate Votes to Confirm William Barr as Next Attorney General Today the Senate voted to confirm William Barr as the next attorney general. Mr. Barr was nominated by President Trump and will be replacing Matthew Whitaker as the acting attorney general. Mr. Barr is succeeding Jeff Sessions, who resigned in early November at the request of Trump.
