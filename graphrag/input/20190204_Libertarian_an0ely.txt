Title: If the Left wants to hold Capitalism responsible for Corporatism, shouldn’t they also hold Socialism responsible for irl examples of “Not real Socialism”?

If the Left wants to hold Capitalism responsible for Corporatism, shouldn’t they also hold Socialism responsible for irl examples of “Not real Socialism”? If they argue that our method gives the opportunity for Corporatism when it is corrupted or abused, the same can be argued for Socialism in places like Venezuela or The USSR. Sure, they’re method was abused and twisted (supposedly), but if they don’t hold themselves responsible for it’s failing, then logically why should we?
