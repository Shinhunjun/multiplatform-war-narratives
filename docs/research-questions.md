# Research Questions

1. How do narratives about Venezuela-US conflict evolve over time across Reddit, GDELT news, and TikTok, and which narrative themes persist, transform, or disappear when moving between traditional media and user-generated discourse?

2. How do significant geopolitical events (i.e., sanctions, elections, protests, and diplomatic or military escalations) change sentiment, topic prevalence, and engagement intensity across platforms, and what are the lag/lead relationships between platform responses?

3. Which actors (states, political leaders, organizations, and communities) are most central in each platform's discourse network, and how do their framing roles differ by platform, ideology, and language community?

4. To what extent can multimodal, multiplatform signals (text sentiment, topics, clusters, and event metadata) be combined to detect narrative polarization and forecast near-term narrative shifts better than any single platform alone?
